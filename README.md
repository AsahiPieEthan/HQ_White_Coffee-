"# HQ_White_Coffee-Website" 

### 🏠 Homepage
The homepage gives a quick overview of HQ White Coffee, including highlights, promotions, and general branding. It’s designed to be simple and easy to navigate so users can quickly get a feel of the website.

### 📖 Menu Page
This page shows the food and drinks available. Items are grouped into categories so users can browse through them more easily without getting overwhelmed.

### ℹ️ About Us
The About Us page shares some background about the brand, including its story and what it represents. It helps users understand the business a bit more.

### 📞 Contact Us
The contact page allows users to reach out or interact with the business directly.
